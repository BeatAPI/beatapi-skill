# Go-to-market diagnosis

Ask for the company website, target country, report language and the decision the reader is trying to make. If the domain or target market is ambiguous, confirm it. Use the reader's language for the whole report. Write for a founder deciding which audience to reach, what to say and where to start.

## Run the research

1. Read the homepage, pricing, a product or use-case page and the sitemap. Identify the product, user, buyer, pricing and path to adoption. Distinguish the company's own claims from your observations. Verify that accounts and domains belong to the same company.
2. Follow the [BeatAPI Skill](https://beatapi.io/skill.md) for setup and the Search → Inspect → Run contract. The agent needs a configured BeatAPI key for paid data calls. Inspect readiness, inputs and price before running within the user's budget. Keep credentials and raw responses out of the HTML.
3. Use [domain SEO research](https://beatapi.io/skill-refs/recipes/seo-domain-research.md) in the selected country. Separate overall visit estimates from organic search estimates. Sample branded and task-based queries, read the actual landing pages, then look for the next step a buyer can take. Record period, country, source and coverage beside each metric.
4. Find official social accounts from the website. Sample recent public posts on one relevant channel and independent mentions of the product. Keep the company's posts, founder posts and third-party mentions separate. Link to representative posts and capture the observation date. Treat missing results as unknown.
5. Choose two relevant competitors by the buyer's task. Compare the specific page, message or demonstration each uses to reach that buyer. Compare search estimates only when their market, period and units match.

## Write one standalone HTML report

Start with the business and its main growth question. Cover search, social activity, competitors and one to three prioritized actions. For each action specify the intended audience, message, channel, supporting evidence and the signal that would justify further investment. Identify attribution, conversion and revenue questions that need first-party data.

Use semantic headings, mobile-friendly tables and links to original sources beside each finding. State the research date, sample limits and which numbers are estimates. Omit unsupported claims and internal logs. Review the result for factual support, usefulness to the founder and readability before handing over the HTML. Deliver the file to the user; public sharing requires their approval.
